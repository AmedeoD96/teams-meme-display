Offline. The most honest status there is.
Signed out, and intending to stay that way.
Not here. Not reachable. Not sorry.
Connection severed. Deliberately.
Offline, which is a lifestyle now.
The laptop is shut. Take the hint.
Unreachable by design, not by accident.
Gone dark. It is a feature.
No presence to report. Blissfully.
Do not call. Do not write. Do not.
